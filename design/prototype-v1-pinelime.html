The signature system

Freshness is a colour, and it decays

One rule, computed server-side from the last verified date. It sorts the feed, colours the badge, and gives agents a reason to keep listings honest.

0 — 7 days

Fresh

Agent re-confirmed within the week. Sits at the top of every feed and wears the lime stamp.

Verified 2d ago
8 — 14 days

Aging

Still shown, but nudged down and flagged ochre. The agent gets a prompt to re-verify.

Verified 11d ago
15 days +

Stale

Dropped from default browse. A seeker's "report unavailable" only accelerates what time already does.
